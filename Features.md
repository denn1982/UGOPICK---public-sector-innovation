# A new yes/no question every 15 minutes 

AI-generated, automatically screened for discriminatory content under Article 1 of the Dutch Constitution (Grondwet).

# Anonymous voting — no account, no name, no email. 

Only a pseudonymous fingerprint is used to prevent double voting.

# Vote choice is never stored  

immediately after voting, only an anonymous counter on the question is incremented.

# Realtime results 

live yes/no bars and percentages, viewable on the Results page.

# Streak tracking  

your voting streak is tracked for the current session (session storage, not persistent).

# English throughout 

built for use by public-sector employees in any country.

# 24/7 availability 

a new question is generated every 15 minutes. The app is built in Europe (Europe/Amsterdam time), but the local timezone may differ in the country where other public-sector employees use it.

# GDPR/AVG-compliant by design 

no tracking cookies, no analytics, EU-hosted fonts, server-side hashing with a secret pepper.
